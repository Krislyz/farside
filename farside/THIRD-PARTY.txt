Programs Farside uses but does not contain:

  usbipd-win (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/dorssel/usbipd-win): shares the controller on the PC you play
  from. Farside Client's Setup fetches it from that project's own release page and installs it.

  usbip-win2 (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/vadimgrn/usbip-win2): receives the controller on the Host PC.
  You install it yourself.
